Packages and payment

Prices are listed in Canadian dollars before HST. Payment is taken online through Square when you buy a package. You receive a receipt by email right after checkout.

Eligibility

You must hold a valid Ontario G1 licence before your first lesson behind the wheel. Please bring it to every lesson.

Scheduling and cancellations

  • We call you within one business day of purchase to book your first lesson.
  • Reschedule or cancel a lesson at least 24 hours in advance at no cost.
  • Lessons cancelled with less notice, or missed, count as completed.

Refunds

Unused packages can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase if no lesson or course module has started. After that, we refund unused lesson hours at the single lesson rate minus any completed course fees.

Road test

DriveTest fees are not included in any package. When your package includes our car for the road test, the booking must be at a DriveTest centre within London.
