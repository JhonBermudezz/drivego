Terms and conditions

Last updated October 22, 2026 · Draft for review by the school before launch

The short version: bring your licence, give us 24 hours' notice if you need to move a lesson, and pay through Square, cash or e-Transfer. The details are below.

1. Packages and prices

Prices are in Canadian dollars and HST is added. Each package page lists exactly what is included. What is not listed is not included.

2. How to pay

You can pay online with Square, in cash, or by e-Transfer. You will get a receipt for every payment.

3. Before your first lesson

  • You need a valid Ontario G1 licence to drive with us.
  • Bring your licence to every lesson. Without it we cannot start the lesson.
  • Tell us about anything that could affect your driving, such as glasses or a medical condition.

4. Booking, moving and cancelling

  • After you buy a package, we contact you within one business day to set your first lesson.
  • You can move or cancel a lesson for free if you tell us at least 24 hours before.
  • With less notice, or if you do not show up, the lesson counts as taken.
  • If we have to cancel because of weather or an instructor emergency, we will offer you a new time at no cost.

5. Refunds

If you change your mind within 14 days and no lesson or course module has started, we refund the full amount. After that, we refund unused lesson hours at the single lesson price, minus any course fees already used.

6. Road test

  • The DriveTest fee is paid to DriveTest and is not part of any package.
  • If your package includes our car for the road test, the test must be at a DriveTest centre in London.
  • We cannot promise you will pass. We prepare you the best we can.

7. Being safe on the road

Your instructor can stop a lesson if it is not safe, for example if you are unwell or have been drinking. That lesson counts as taken.

8. Questions

Write to info@drivegolondon.ca and we will help.
