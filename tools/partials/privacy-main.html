Privacy policy

Effective October 25, 2026 · Last updated October 25, 2026

In short: we collect only what we need to teach you to drive, we never sell your personal information, and you can ask to see, correct or delete it at any time.

1. Who we are

Drive Go London Driving School ("Drive Go London", "we", "us") is a driving school in London, Ontario, Canada approved by the Ministry of Transportation of Ontario (MTO) to deliver the Beginner Driver Education (BDE) course. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect personal information when you visit drivegodrivingschool.com, contact us, book lessons or buy a package. It follows Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Ontario law.

Our privacy contact is Steven Bernal, founder. Write to info@drivegodrivingschool.com or call (519) 694 3762.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone number and the messages you send us through the website form, email, phone or WhatsApp.
  • Student information: licence class and number, date of birth and address, when needed to register you for the BDE course, schedule lessons or book a road test.
  • Payment information: card payments are processed by Square. We never see or store your full card number. For cash and Interac e-Transfer we keep only a record that you paid and the amount.
  • Website data: technical information such as your browser type, device, pages viewed and approximate location, collected through our hosting and security tools.

3. Why we collect it

  • To answer your questions and schedule your lessons, course and road test.
  • To register your BDE course completion with the MTO and issue your certificate.
  • To take payments, issue receipts and keep financial records.
  • To send lesson reminders and service messages related to your booking.
  • To keep our website secure and working well.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We ask for your consent before collecting, using or disclosing your personal information, except where the law allows or requires otherwise. By contacting us or buying a service you agree to the uses described here. You can withdraw consent at any time, subject to legal and contractual limits.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with:

  • The Ministry of Transportation of Ontario (MTO): the information required to register your BDE course.
  • Square: to process card payments and, where used, online booking.
  • Our instructors: the details needed to teach you and plan your lessons.
  • Service providers: website hosting, email and form delivery providers that help us run the site, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities: when required by law, court order or to protect safety.

Some of these providers, such as Square and our hosting company, may store data outside Canada, where it can be subject to the laws of that country.

5. Cookies and third party content

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded Google Map, and links to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. These services may set their own cookies or receive your IP address under their own privacy policies. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

6. Email and text messages

We send messages only about the lessons, courses and services you requested. We do not send marketing email or text messages without your express consent, in line with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL). Every message gives you a way to opt out.

7. How long we keep it

We keep student records for as long as the MTO requires driving schools to keep them, and financial records for the period required by tax law (generally six years). Messages that do not lead to a booking are deleted within a reasonable time. When information is no longer needed we delete or anonymize it securely.

8. How we protect it

We use reasonable safeguards, including access limited to authorized staff, secure payment processing and encrypted connections (HTTPS) on this website. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, to give you access to it, to correct it, or to delete it, and you can withdraw your consent. Email info@drivegodrivingschool.com and we will answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

10. Children and young drivers

Many of our students are teenagers. When a student is under 18 we ask a parent or guardian to contact us and consent to the collection and use of the student's information.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version. If we make an important change we will make it clear on this page.
