About us

A driving school built around you

Drive Go London was founded by Steven Bernal, a certified driving instructor and former DriveTest examiner. He knows exactly what examiners look for, and he teaches every student to pass with that insight. Steven leads every program and works alongside two more MTO certified instructors.

We are proud to be the first Hispanic driving school in London approved by the MTO as a BDE course provider, with lessons in English and Spanish.

We keep classes small, pick you up at home or meet you at White Oaks Mall, and plan every lesson around the test routes you will actually drive in London and surrounding areas.

FAQ

Questions before your first lesson

Still not sure? Call us at (519) 694 3762.

Do I need my G1 before I start?

You need a valid G1 for lessons behind the wheel. You can start the BDE classroom course before you have it.

How does the BDE course get me to my G2 sooner?

Completing an MTO approved Beginner Driver Education course lets you take your G2 road test after 8 months with your G1 instead of 12.

Where do lessons start?

With the Basic package we meet you at White Oaks Mall. Standard and Complete include pick up and drop off at home, school or work in London.

Can I use your car for my road test?

Yes. The Complete package includes it, and you can add it to any plan with a road test package for G2 or G.

Can I take lessons in Spanish?

Yes. Steven teaches in English and Spanish, and our BDE course is available for Spanish speakers too.

How can I pay?

Pay online with any credit or debit card through Stripe, or pay by Interac e‑Transfer or cash before your first lesson.

What if I need to cancel a lesson?

Reschedule or cancel at least 24 hours before your lesson at no cost. See our terms for full refund details.
