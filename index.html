About us

A driving school built around you

Drive Go London is a family run school teaching new drivers across London, St. Thomas and Strathroy since 2017. Every instructor is MTO certified, background checked and trained to teach nervous first timers.

We keep classes small, pick you up from home, school or work, and plan every lesson around the G2 test routes you will actually drive.
