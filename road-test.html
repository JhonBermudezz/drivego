Test day

How test day works

  1. We pick you up

    90 minutes before your test, from home, school or work.

  2. Warm up drive

    We drive the roads around the DriveTest centre so nothing surprises you.

  3. Your test

    You take the test in the car you already practised in.

  4. Back home

    We drop you off and go over the examiner notes together.

From a former examiner

What examiners look for

Steven spent years on the other side of the clipboard as a DriveTest examiner. These are the habits he trains first.

  • Full stops behind the line, every time.
  • Shoulder checks before every lane change and turn.
  • Safe following distance of at least two seconds.
  • Smooth speed control in school and construction zones.

What to bring

  • Your valid G1 or G2 licence
  • Glasses or contacts if your licence requires them
  • Your DriveTest booking confirmation
  • A payment method for DriveTest fees
