Privacy policy

Last updated October 22, 2026 · Draft for review by the school before launch

The short version: we only ask for what we need to teach you to drive, we never sell your information, and you can ask us to see or delete it at any time.

1. Who we are

Drive Go London is an MTO-approved driving school in London, Ontario. Questions about your privacy go to info@drivegolondon.ca.

2. What we collect

  • When you write to us or book: your name, email, phone number, licence level and the message or answers you send.
  • When you pay: Square processes card payments. We never see or store your full card number. If you pay by cash or e-Transfer, we only keep a record that you paid.
  • When you visit this website: basic technical data such as browser type and pages viewed, so the site keeps working well.

3. Why we use it

  • To book and run your lessons, your course and your road test.
  • To register your Beginner Driver Education completion with the MTO.
  • To send receipts, reminders and answers to your questions.

4. Who we share it with

  • The Ministry of Transportation (MTO): only what it requires to register your course.
  • Square: only what it needs to take your payment.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell or rent your information.

5. How long we keep it

We keep student records for as long as the MTO requires driving schools to keep them. After that we delete them securely. Messages that never turn into a booking are deleted within a reasonable time.

6. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The embedded Google Map may set its own cookies, under Google's policy.

7. Your rights

Under Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA) you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or take back your consent. Email info@drivegolondon.ca and we will answer within 30 days.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
