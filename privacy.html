Privacy policy

Last updated October 21, 2026 · Draft for review by the school before launch

What we collect

When you contact us or buy a package we collect your name, email, phone number, licence level and the message you send. Payments are processed by Square. We never see or store your full card number.

How we use it

  • To schedule your lessons and your BDE course.
  • To issue your MTO course completion certificate.
  • To send receipts and lesson reminders.

Who we share it with

We share the information the Ministry of Transportation requires to register your Beginner Driver Education completion. Square receives your payment details to process your order. We do not sell your information.

How long we keep it

We keep student records for the period the MTO requires driving schools to retain them, then delete them securely.

Your rights

Under PIPEDA you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information. Email info@drivegolondon.ca and we will reply within 30 days.
