2. Our services

We offer the MTO approved Beginner Driver Education (BDE) course, in car lessons, road test packages and refresher lessons. Each package or service page lists what is included. Anything not listed is not included. We may change services and prices at any time, but a change does not affect a package you have already paid for.

3. Eligibility and what to bring

  • You must hold a valid Ontario G1 or G2 licence, as required for the lesson, before any lesson behind the wheel.
  • Bring your licence to every lesson. We cannot start a lesson without it.
  • Wear your glasses or contacts if your licence requires them.
  • Tell your instructor about any medical or other condition that could affect your driving.

4. Prices and payment

  • All prices are in Canadian dollars. HST is added unless stated otherwise.
  • You can pay online by card through Square, by Interac e-Transfer, or in cash. You will receive a receipt for every payment.
  • Packages and lessons must be paid before or at the time of booking unless we agree otherwise in writing.
  • Government fees, such as DriveTest road test fees, are paid by you directly to the government and are not included in our prices.

5. Booking, rescheduling and cancellation

  • After you buy a package we contact you within one business day to schedule your first lesson.
  • You may reschedule or cancel a lesson free of charge if you tell us at least 24 hours before it starts.
  • If you cancel with less than 24 hours' notice, or do not show up, the lesson counts as taken and is not refunded.
  • If we must cancel because of bad weather, instructor illness or an emergency, we will offer you a new time at no cost.
  • Please be ready at the agreed place. If you are more than 15 minutes late the lesson may be shortened or counted as taken.

6. Refunds

If you change your mind within 14 days of buying a package and no lesson or course module has started, we will refund the full amount. After that, we refund unused lesson hours at the single lesson price, minus any course fees already used. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

7. Road tests

  • You book your road test directly with DriveTest. Send us the date and time so we can plan your pick up and warm up.
  • When your package includes our car for the road test, the test must take place at a DriveTest centre in London. If DriveTest changes your date we will reschedule at no extra cost.
  • We prepare you as well as we can, but we cannot guarantee that you will pass your road test or obtain a licence.

  • Your instructor may end a lesson if it is unsafe, for example if you are unwell, impaired by alcohol, cannabis or medication, or behave abusively. That lesson counts as taken.

  • You are responsible for any traffic fines or penalties that result from your own actions.
