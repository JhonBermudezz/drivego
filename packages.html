The BDE course, explained

Beginner Driver Education is the Ministry of Transportation program that turns a G1 into a confident, test ready driver. Drive Go London is an MTO approved BDE course provider.

  • 30 hours of online learning at your own pace, anytime, anywhere.
  • 10 hours of in car training with a certified instructor.
  • Official MTO BDE certificate when you finish.
  • G2 road test after 8 months with your G1 instead of 12.
  • Lower insurance rates with many Ontario insurers.

Good to know

Do I need my G1 before I start?

You need a valid G1 for lessons behind the wheel. You can start the online BDE course before you have it.

Can I pay in instalments or in cash?

Pay online with any credit or debit card through Square, or pay by Interac e‑Transfer or cash before your first lesson. Ask us about splitting the payment.

Can I upgrade my package later?

Yes. Pay the difference and we add the extra hours, home pick up or the car for your test.

What happens after I pay?

We call or WhatsApp you within one business day to schedule your first lesson and send your BDE course access.

Not sure which package fits?

Tell us your licence level and we will recommend one.
